# Match Notes — Meta Technical Solutions Consultant (New York, NY)

Notes on how the tailored resume/cover letter map to the job posting, plus honest gaps to be ready
to address in screens/interviews. Nothing below fabricates experience you don't have — Meta's own
ad platform, Conversions API, and Pixel are called out as gaps rather than skills.

## Strong matches (minimum qualifications)

- **Degree:** B.Tech in CS + MS in CS — satisfies "B.A./B.S. in Business, CS, Engineering, ... or
  equivalent practical experience."
- **4+ years technical consulting / TAM-style role supporting external partners:** ~4 years at
  HCLTech as the primary technical point of contact for FedEx (Analyst → Senior Analyst), plus
  Graduate Assistant stakeholder work.
- **4+ years working with web/mobile APIs:** REST API troubleshooting, request/response and log
  analysis, integration support, FastAPI work on the Monish Labs project.
- **Analyzing complex problems → clear recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences:**
  root-cause analysis across app/DB/infra/network layers, then stakeholder communication.
- **Cross-functional collaboration (product/engineering/GTM-style teams):** coordinated releases
  and post-launch support with application owners and engineering teams; less direct experience
  with formal "go-to-market" teams specifically — frame HCLTech's business-stakeholder coordination
  as the closest analog.
- **Data/metrics to evaluate solution effectiveness:** 70% manual-effort reduction, 15+ hrs/week
  saved, 40% faster resolution, 99.9% availability — all quantified and stakeholder-communicated.
- **Client engagement across org layers:** Strong with application owners/engineering/ops;
  **C-suite exposure is the thinnest point** — be ready to discuss any instance of presenting to
  senior stakeholders, or reframe FedEx engagement scope/visibility if it reached that level.

## Strong matches (preferred qualifications)

- **Integration playbooks / troubleshooting guides / developer-facing documentation:** direct hit —
  authored and published implementation/troubleshooting guides to an internal knowledge base.
- **Integrating AI tools to redesign workflows with measurable impact:** direct hit — AI-integrated
  workflows at Indiana State University and in the Monish Labs project, framed as reducing manual
  effort/errors.
- **Ongoing AI skill development:** reasonable to speak to in interview (tools/workflows used,
  prompt/context engineering practices), even though the resume doesn't itemize specific tools.

## Gaps to acknowledge, not fabricate

- **No direct experience with Meta's advertising platform, Conversions API, or Meta Pixel.** The
  resume/cover letter intentionally avoid claiming this. In interviews, pivot to the *transferable*
  skill: diagnosing signal/data-flow issues and API integrations in general (FedEx systems), and
  express willingness/aptitude to ramp quickly on Meta-specific tooling.
  - If you want to close this gap before applying, doing a short hands-on pass with the Meta
    Conversions API / Pixel docs (even a toy integration) would give you something concrete and
    honest to reference.
- **Attribution modeling, signal quality optimization, privacy-preserving data practices:** not
  currently represented. Same approach — acknowledge as an area you're ramping on if asked directly,
  and lean on your root-cause-analysis and data-pipeline background as the closest adjacent
  strength.
- **Responsible/ethical AI practices (risk assessment, bias mitigation, QA reviews):** not
  explicitly evidenced in current bullets. If you've done any validation/QA around your AI-integrated
  workflows (e.g., checking automation output before it reaches a partner), that's fair to mention
  in an interview even though it's understated on paper.

## Suggested next steps

1. Use `resume.md` as the source of truth for a tailored resume; port it into your existing
   resume template/design so formatting matches your original PDF.
2. Personalize `cover-letter.md` with the hiring manager's name if you can find one, and adjust the
   closing paragraph if you have a specific reason for wanting to join Meta (product you use,
   team you've researched, etc.) — a specific hook usually reads stronger than a general one.
3. Prep 2-3 STAR stories from the FedEx engagement that cover: (a) a hard root-cause diagnosis,
   (b) a time you built something (tool/guide) that scaled beyond one ticket, and (c) a time you
   used metrics to justify or measure a solution's impact.
