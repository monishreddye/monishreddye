I am eager to bring that same AI-accelerated approach to root-cause analysis and partner communications for Meta's advertising and platform products --- including signals, measurement, and ads delivery --- and to deepen my expertise in the Conversions API, Meta Pixel, and privacy-preserving measurement.

Meta's scale means every diagnosed root cause, every playbook, and every automation compounds across thousands of partners. I would welcome the chance to help partners grow on that scale. Thank you for your consideration; I look forward to speaking with you.
